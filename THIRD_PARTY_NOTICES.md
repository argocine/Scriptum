# Third-party notices

Scriptum is built with [Electron](https://www.electronjs.org/), which includes
Chromium and Node.js. Their license texts and the notices for Chromium's
third-party components are shipped beside the installed application in its
`licenses` resources directory:

- `ELECTRON-LICENSE.txt`
- `LICENSES.chromium.html`

Scriptum bundles regular-weight Noto Sans Mono, Noto Sans Mono CJK SC, Noto
Sans Devanagari, Noto Sans Arabic, and Noto Sans Hebrew fonts for dependable
offline PDF output. The fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1. Their complete license texts are shipped in the same `licenses`
directory:

- `OFL-Noto.txt`
- `OFL-Noto-CJK.txt`

Scriptum's own source code is distributed under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.
